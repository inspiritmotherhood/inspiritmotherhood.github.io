Welcome to In Spirit Motherhood-a place of faith, family, purpose, and encouragement. Here I share my journey with God, Christian writing, books, and words of hope for those walking through every season of life.

About Me

My name is Freweyni. I am a mother, Christian author, and woman of faith whose life has been shaped by God's grace. Through every season of my journey, I have learned to trust His Word, walk by faith, and find purpose in serving and encouraging others.

My Books

My writing is rooted in faith and a desire to point readers toward Jesus. Through my books, I share messages of hope, spiritual growth, prayer, God's Word, and the transforming power of His presence.

In Spirit Motherhood

In Spirit Motherhood is a faith-centered ministry created to encourage, strengthen, and inspire women and families through faith, motherhood, testimony, and the Word of God.

This is a place to grow in faith, find encouragement through life's journeys, and remember that God can use every season of our lives for His purpose and His glory.

Through Christian books, personal testimony, prayer, and messages of hope, In Spirit Motherhood points hearts toward Jesus Christ and the transforming power of His Word.
