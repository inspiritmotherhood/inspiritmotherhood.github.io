In Spirit Motherhood

In Spirit Motherhood is a faith-centered ministry created to encourage, strengthen, and inspire women and families through faith, motherhood, testimony, and the Word of God.

This is a place to grow in faith, find encouragement through life's journeys, and remember that God can use every season of our lives for His purpose and His glory.

Through Christian books, personal testimony, prayer, and messages of hope, In Spirit Motherhood points hearts toward Jesus Christ and the transforming power of His Word.

Messages of Faith & Encouragement

When God Sees You in the Hidden Season

There are seasons when we may feel unseen by people, overlooked, or uncertain about what God is doing in our lives. Yet being hidden from the eyes of people does not mean we are hidden from God.

God sees the mother who keeps praying. He sees the person who keeps serving when no one applauds. He sees every tear, every sacrifice, every quiet act of faith, and every moment we choose to trust Him when we cannot yet see the outcome.

The hidden season is not an empty season. God can use it to prepare our hearts, strengthen our faith, and teach us to depend on Him. What feels like waiting may become preparation for the purpose He has already placed before us.

So if you are walking through a hidden season today, remember: God sees you. Keep praying. Keep believing. Keep walking according to His Word. Your life is in the hands of the One who sees the beginning, the middle, and the end.

"You are the God who sees me." — Genesis 16:13

When God Says Wait

Waiting can be one of the most difficult parts of walking by faith. We pray, we believe, and sometimes we expect God to answer immediately. But there are moments when His answer is not "no." His answer is simply, "Wait."

Waiting does not mean that God has forgotten us. It does not mean that our prayers have gone unheard. Sometimes God is working in places we cannot see, preparing circumstances, preparing people, and most importantly, preparing us.

We often see only what is in front of us, but God sees the whole journey. He knows what we need before we arrive at the place He is taking us. What feels like a delay to us may be part of His preparation.

Think about a seed planted beneath the soil. For a season, nothing can be seen above the ground. Yet underneath, something is happening. Roots are forming. Life is developing. The seed is being prepared to break through the soil at the appointed time.

Our waiting seasons can be like that.

Maybe you have been praying for your family. Maybe you are waiting for a door to open, an answer to come, a promise to be fulfilled, or direction for your next step. Do not measure God's faithfulness by how quickly your circumstances change.

Continue praying.
Continue trusting.
Continue obeying.
Continue walking according to His Word.

Faith is not only believing God when we receive the answer. Faith is also trusting His character while we are still waiting for it.

God may use the waiting season to strengthen what will be necessary for the next season. And when the door finally opens, we may understand why He did not allow us to walk through it earlier.

So when God says, "Wait," do not mistake His timing for His absence.

He is still God in the waiting.
He is still faithful in the silence.
And He is still working even when you cannot yet see what He is doing.

"Wait on the LORD: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart." — Psalm 27:14 (KJV)

Prayer

Heavenly Father, teach me to trust You while I wait. When I cannot see what You are doing, help me remember who You are. Strengthen my heart, guide my steps, and keep me faithful to Your Word. Give me patience not to run ahead of You and faith not to give up before Your appointed time. Let my waiting season draw me closer to You and prepare me for whatever You have called me to do.

In Jesus' name, Amen.

When God Opens the Door

There are seasons when we pray for a door to open. We wait, we trust, and we wonder when the answer will come. Then one day, something changes. An opportunity appears. A prayer begins to unfold. The door we have been standing before suddenly opens.

But an open door is not only an opportunity to celebrate. It is also an invitation to walk forward in faith.

Sometimes we spend so much time asking God to open the door that when He finally does, fear makes us hesitate.

We may ask, "Am I ready?"
"Am I qualified?"
"What if I fail?"
"What if I cannot handle what is on the other side?"

But if God has prepared the way, we do not have to enter depending only on our own strength. The same God who sustained us in the hidden season and strengthened us in the waiting season will be with us when it is time to move.

The waiting was not wasted.
The prayers were not wasted.
The lessons were not wasted.

Even the difficult seasons can become part of the preparation God uses for what comes next.

Yet not every open door is necessarily God's direction. This is why we still pray. We still seek wisdom. We still compare what is before us with the Word of God. We should not run through a door simply because it is available. We want to walk through the doors that align with God's will.

And when He makes the way clear, faith requires movement.

There comes a moment when we must stop standing at the doorway and take the next step.

Maybe God is calling you to serve.
Maybe He is giving you an opportunity you once prayed for.
Maybe He is asking you to use your testimony, your gifts, your education, your experience, or even the lessons from your pain to help someone else.

Do not allow fear to make the decision that belongs to faith.

Walk humbly.
Walk prayerfully.
Walk courageously.

And remember who opened the door.

When God blesses us with an opportunity, the purpose is not simply for us to become greater. Let the opportunity become another place where His goodness can be seen, His love can be shared, and His name can be glorified.

The door may be new, but the One leading you through it has been faithful all along.

So when God opens the door, thank Him for the waiting that prepared you, ask Him for wisdom for what lies ahead, and take the next step with faith.

"I have set before thee an open door, and no man can shut it." — Revelation 3:8 (KJV)

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for every door You open according to Your will. Give me wisdom to recognize the opportunities that come from You and discernment to walk away from those that do not. When You call me forward, do not let fear keep me standing at the doorway. Give me courage to obey You, humility to depend on You, and wisdom to honor You with every opportunity You place before me.

May every door You open in my life become a place where Your purpose is fulfilled and Your name is glorified.

In Jesus' name, Amen.
